Traveler's Notes

  • Remember to drink responsibly and consider a designated driver or tour service for each day's activities.
  • Tours should be booked in advance as many distilleries are very popular, especially on weekends.
  • January weather in Kentucky can be unpredictable - pack layers and check forecasts before heading out each day.
  • Most distilleries offer non-alcoholic options for designated drivers.
  • Take your time at each location to fully appreciate the craftsmanship and history.
  • Consider bringing a small notebook to record tasting notes and preferences.
  • Most distilleries have gift shops where you can purchase bottles and souvenirs.
  • With 6 Bourbon Stewards traveling, consider vehicle logistics - either two cars or a larger SUV/van for the group.
  • Travel from Webster's Chapel, AL to Frankfort, KY: approximately 6 hours (380 miles).
  • Total driving for Friday: approximately 2 hours 30 minutes (95 miles).
  • Total driving for Saturday: approximately 3 hours 45 minutes (105 miles).
